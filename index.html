ℹ️ How to Use

🎨 Drawing: Draw on the large canvas using the pen tool. Select colors and adjust brush size.

🎥 Paper Detection: Point your camera at physical paper with lines. The system detects lines based on your color selection.

🔍 Color Detection: Choose a color (black, blue, red, green) and the camera will only detect that color on the paper.

📄 Lined Paper Mode: Enable this to detect horizontal lines on ruled paper and only track NEW lines you draw.

↔️ Flip: Flip your canvas drawing horizontally (X) or vertically (Y) without affecting camera feed.

🗑️ Eraser: Switch to eraser mode to remove parts of your drawing.

⬇️ Download: Save your drawing as a PNG image.
