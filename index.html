ℹ️ How to Use

🎨 Drawing: Click and drag on the canvas to draw. Select different tools and colors using the controls above.

🎥 Camera: Your camera feed will appear on the left. Grant permission when prompted.

🔍 Line Detection: Choose a color to detect and track lines in real-time from the camera feed. Detected lines will be highlighted.

📄 Lined Paper: Enable line detection to automatically detect and filter lined paper patterns.

↔️ Flip Options: Flip your drawing or camera feed horizontally (X-axis) or vertically (Y-axis).

🗑️ Eraser: Switch to eraser mode to remove parts of your drawing. Click "Clear All" to remove everything.

⬇️ Download: Save your drawing as a PNG image.

↶ Undo: Revert your last drawing action.
